# Business Case  
## InsightCare Analytics

**Version**: 1.0

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Fragmented reporting and inconsistent metrics slow decision-making and erode trust in data. By delivering a governed healthcare analytics dashboard suite focused on high-value operational, quality, and financial KPIs, the organization can improve situational awareness, reduce time spent on manual reporting, and support faster, better decisions.

---

### 2. Problem Statement

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Multiple versions of the “same” metric | Confusion, debates in meetings instead of action |
| Delayed operational visibility | Reactive management of capacity, flow, and staffing |
| Heavy reliance on analysts for ad-hoc extracts | Analyst bottleneck; delayed insights |
| Limited self-service | Leaders wait for reports instead of exploring data |
| Poor auditability of numbers | Low trust; repeated reconciliation effort |

---

### 3. Proposed Solution

A layered analytics solution:

1. **Governed KPI Layer** – Agreed definitions, owners, calculation rules  
2. **Semantic / Data Model** – Consistent, documented measures  
3. **Role-Based Dashboards** – Executive, Operations (Flow & Capacity), Quality, Finance views  
4. **Validation & Trust Framework** – Documented reconciliation and user acceptance  
5. **Enablement** – Training and self-service support  

Primary delivery tool assumed: **Power BI** (or equivalent modern BI platform).

---

### 4. Expected Benefits

- Faster access to trusted operational KPIs (daily vs month-end)
- Reduced time spent by analysts on repetitive reporting
- Improved meeting effectiveness (discussion shifts from “whose number is right?” to “what should we do?”)
- Better capacity and flow management through timely visibility
- Stronger data culture and foundation for future advanced analytics

---

### 5. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Continue current Excel + ad-hoc | Familiar | Unsustainable, inconsistent | Rejected |
| Buy multiple point solutions | Fast for single domains | Fragmentation continues | Rejected |
| Full enterprise DW + analytics program | Comprehensive | Long, expensive, high risk | Phased later |
| **Focused dashboard suite on priority KPIs (Selected)** | Faster value, manageable scope | Limited breadth initially | **Recommended** |

---

### 6. Recommendation

Proceed with a focused Healthcare Analytics Dashboard initiative centered on a prioritized set of operational, quality, and financial KPIs, delivered primarily through Power BI with strong governance and validation.
