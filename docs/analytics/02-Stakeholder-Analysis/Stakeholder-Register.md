### Insights
- Executive and operational leaders are the primary value buyers.
- Unit managers and ED leaders will drive daily adoption — metrics must be actionable and trusted.
- Analysts who currently produce Excel reports need a clear transition path.
- Data and BI teams must be co-owners of feasibility and sustainability.
