# Business Requirements  
## InsightCare Analytics

**Version**: 1.0

---

### 1. Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| BR-01 | The organization shall have a single, governed definition for each Phase 1 KPI | Must |
| BR-02 | Operational leaders shall have daily visibility into patient flow and capacity KPIs | Must |
| BR-03 | Executive leadership shall have a concise institutional performance view | Must |
| BR-04 | Finance and RCM leaders shall have timely visibility into AR, clean claim, and denial metrics | Must |
| BR-05 | Quality leaders shall have visibility into agreed quality & safety indicators | Must |
| BR-06 | Users shall be able to filter and drill from summary to supporting detail within agreed boundaries | Must |
| BR-07 | Metric calculations shall be transparent and documented | Must |
| BR-08 | Access to dashboards shall be controlled by role | Must |
| BR-09 | Dashboards shall display the “Data as of” timestamp and any known data limitations | Must |
| BR-10 | The solution shall reduce reliance on manual Excel consolidation for the in-scope metrics | Should |

---

### 2. Reporting Requirements (Summary)

- **Executive Pack**: High-level KPIs + trends (daily/weekly/monthly views)
- **Operational Flow Pack**: ED and Inpatient capacity & flow (daily)
- **Quality Snapshot**: Selected indicators (monthly with trends)
- **Finance / RCM Snapshot**: AR days, clean claim, denial rate (daily/weekly)
- Ability to export underlying summarized data for further analysis (within policy)
