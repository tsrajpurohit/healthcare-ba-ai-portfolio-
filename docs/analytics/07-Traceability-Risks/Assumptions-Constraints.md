# Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies  
## InsightCare Analytics

### Assumptions
- Business owners will participate in KPI definition and sign-off
- Source systems can provide the required data at the needed grain and frequency
- Power BI (or agreed BI tool) is the delivery platform
- Users have appropriate devices and network access
- A governance process will continue after go-live for metric changes

### Constraints
- Phase 1 limited to prioritized KPIs and defined dashboards
- Privacy and security policies must be respected in all aggregates and access rules
- Timeline driven by leadership need for better operational visibility
- Existing data platform capabilities constrain real-time vs daily refresh options

### Dependencies
- Availability of data extracts or warehouse tables
- Metric owner engagement for definitions and validation
- BI development capacity
- Identity and access management support for role-based security
- Change management support for adoption
