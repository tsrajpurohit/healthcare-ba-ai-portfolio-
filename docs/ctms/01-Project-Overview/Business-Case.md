# Business Case  
## TrialFlow CTMS

**Version**: 1.0

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Clinical trial operational inefficiency and compliance risk represent significant cost and timeline drivers. By implementing a focused Clinical Trial Management System covering study setup, site activation, subject enrollment, and visit tracking, the organization can reduce cycle times, improve data reliability, and strengthen inspection readiness.

Conservative estimates indicate meaningful reductions in administrative effort and faster study startup, supporting both operational excellence and regulatory confidence.

---

### 2. Problem Statement

| Area | Current Pain | Impact |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Site Activation | Manual feasibility, email-based tracking, version confusion | Delayed first-patient-in |
| Enrollment Tracking | Spreadsheets per study/site, delayed updates | Inaccurate forecasts, missed enrollment targets |
| Visit Management | Calendar + email reminders, no single source of truth | Missed visits, protocol deviations |
| Compliance & Audit | Documents scattered, limited audit trails | High preparation effort, inspection risk |
| Cross-Study Visibility | Manual consolidation | Slow decision-making by leadership |

---

### 3. Proposed Solution

Implement **TrialFlow CTMS** – a centralized system for core clinical trial operational workflows with:

- Structured study and protocol management
- Standardized site feasibility and activation workflows
- Subject lifecycle tracking (screening → consent → enrollment → visits)
- Configurable visit schedules and status tracking
- Role-based access and comprehensive electronic audit trails
- Operational dashboards and standard reports

The solution will be designed with regulatory expectations in mind (audit trails, user attribution, data integrity principles).

---

### 4. Expected Benefits

**Quantitative (Indicative)**
- Reduction in site activation cycle time: 25–35%
- Reduction in time spent on enrollment status reporting: 40–60%
- Reduction in audit preparation effort: significant (weeks → days)
- Fewer protocol deviations related to visit window misses

**Qualitative**
- Single source of truth for study operational status
- Improved collaboration between study teams and sites
- Stronger inspection readiness posture
- Scalable foundation for future EDC / eTMF integrations

---

### 5. High-Level Cost Categories

- Software license / subscription
- Implementation & configuration
- Data migration & cleansing
- Validation / testing support
- Training & change management
- Ongoing support

---

### 6. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Continue with spreadsheets + email | Low immediate cost | High risk, not scalable | Rejected |
| Expand existing fragmented tools | Familiarity | Technical debt, limited capability | Rejected |
| Full enterprise CTMS + EDC + eTMF suite | Comprehensive | High cost, long timeline, high complexity | Deferred |
| **Focused CTMS for core operational workflows (Selected)** | Faster value, manageable scope, compliance foundation | Limited clinical data capture initially | **Recommended** |

---

### 7. Recommendation

Proceed with a focused Clinical Trial Management System covering study setup, site management, subject enrollment, and visit tracking. This delivers tangible operational and compliance benefits while establishing a controlled platform for future expansion.
