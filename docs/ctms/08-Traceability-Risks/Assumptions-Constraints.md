# Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies  
## TrialFlow CTMS

### Assumptions
- Clinical Operations leadership will actively sponsor process change and site engagement
- Study Managers, CRAs, and selected Site Coordinators will be available for workshops and UAT
- Sites have basic internet access and browser capability
- Organizational authentication standards can be applied
- Controlled terminology (statuses, reasons) can be agreed early
- Future integration with EDC/eTMF is desired but not required for Phase 1 success

### Constraints
- Must support data integrity and auditability expectations of clinical research
- Phase 1 scope strictly limited (no full EDC, IWRS, or eTMF content management)
- Usability for non-technical site staff is critical
- Changes after go-live must follow controlled processes
- Timeline driven by upcoming study needs

### Dependencies
- Availability of subject matter experts for process validation
- Timely decisions on status values, visit window rules, and document checklists
- IT support for infrastructure, security, and future interfaces
- QA/Validation involvement for compliance alignment
- Site willingness to adopt the system for the pilot studies
