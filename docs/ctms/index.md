### Project Summary

**Project Name**: TrialFlow CTMS – Clinical Trial Management System  
**Focus Areas**:  
- Study / Protocol Setup & Configuration  
- Site Identification, Feasibility & Activation  
- Subject Screening, Informed Consent & Enrollment  
- Visit Scheduling & Tracking  
- Adverse Event / Safety Reporting (high-level)  
- Monitoring, Reporting & Audit Readiness  

**Business Context**: A mid-sized biotech / CRO / research hospital network conducting Phase II–III trials across multiple sites. Current processes rely heavily on spreadsheets, email, shared drives, and fragmented systems, leading to delays in site activation, enrollment tracking issues, compliance risks, and poor real-time visibility.

**Core Value**: Centralize and digitize core clinical trial operational workflows while maintaining strict regulatory compliance (ICH-GCP, FDA 21 CFR Part 11 concepts, audit trails).

---

### Key Outcomes Targeted

| Metric | Current Challenge | Target |
|--------|-------------------|--------|
| Site Activation Cycle Time | Long & variable | Reduce by 25–35% |
| Subject Enrollment Tracking Accuracy | Manual / error-prone | Near real-time, ≥ 98% accuracy |
| Protocol Deviation Visibility | Delayed / incomplete | Real-time flagging |
| Audit Preparation Effort | High (weeks of gathering) | Significantly reduced via system audit trails |
| Cross-site Visibility | Limited | Centralized dashboards |
