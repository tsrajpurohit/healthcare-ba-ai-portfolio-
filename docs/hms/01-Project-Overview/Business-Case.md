# Business Case  
## CityCare General Hospital – HMS Core Modules

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: August 2025

---

### 1. Executive Summary

CityCare General Hospital faces operational inefficiencies in patient registration, appointment management, and billing that negatively impact patient experience, staff productivity, and revenue realization.  

Implementing an integrated digital solution for these three areas is expected to deliver significant quantitative and qualitative benefits within 12 months of go-live, with a projected payback period of 14–18 months.

---

### 2. Current Situation (Problem Statement)

| Area | Current Pain Points | Quantified Impact (Estimated) |
|------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Registration | Paper forms + manual search, long queues | Avg. 12–18 min per patient; high duplicate records (~8–12%) |
| Appointments | Phone/Excel based, no reminders | No-show rate ~22–25%; lost revenue & idle slots |
| Billing | Manual charge entry, delayed invoices | Revenue leakage, delayed collections, high rework |
| Overall | Fragmented data, no real-time visibility | Management decisions based on outdated reports |

---

### 3. Proposed Solution

Implement a modern Hospital Management System focused on:

1. Intelligent Patient Registration with Master Patient Index (MPI)
2. Real-time multi-provider Appointment Scheduling with automated reminders
3. Automated Billing & Payment Collection with basic insurance support

The solution will be cloud or hybrid, role-based, and designed for high usability by non-technical staff.

---

### 4. Expected Benefits

**Quantitative Benefits (Annualized Estimates)**

| Benefit Category | Estimated Annual Value | Assumptions |
|------------------|------------------------|-----------|
| Reduced registration staff overtime / better throughput | ₹18–25 Lakhs | Higher patient volume handled with same staff |
| Reduced no-shows (better utilization) | ₹35–50 Lakhs | 8–10% absolute reduction in no-shows |
| Improved billing accuracy & faster collections | ₹20–30 Lakhs | Reduced leakage + faster cash conversion |
| Reduced paper & manual rework | ₹5–8 Lakhs | Printing, storage, error correction |
| **Total Estimated Annual Benefit** | **₹78–113 Lakhs** | Conservative range |

**Qualitative Benefits**
- Significantly improved patient experience and NPS
- Better staff satisfaction (less frustration with manual work)
- Real-time operational dashboards for leadership
- Strong foundation for future clinical modules and analytics
- Improved compliance and audit readiness

---

### 6. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Recommendation |
|--------|------|------|----------------|
| A. Do Nothing | Zero cost | Problems worsen with volume growth | Rejected |
| B. Enhance Legacy System | Lower short-term cost | Technical debt, limited capability | Rejected |
| C. Full Enterprise HIS (all modules) | Comprehensive | High cost, long timeline, high risk | Deferred to Phase 2+ |
| **D. Focused Core Modules (Selected)** | Faster ROI, manageable risk, clear scope | Limited clinical features initially | **Recommended** |

---

### 7. Risks & Mitigation (Summary)

- User adoption risk → Strong change management + early involvement
- Data quality risk → Robust MPI matching rules + cleansing
- Vendor delivery risk → Clear SLAs + phased milestones
- Scope creep → Strict change control board

---

### 8. Recommendation

Proceed with the focused implementation of Patient Registration, Appointment Scheduling, and Billing modules. The business case shows strong potential for positive ROI within 18 months while delivering immediate improvements in patient experience and operational efficiency.
