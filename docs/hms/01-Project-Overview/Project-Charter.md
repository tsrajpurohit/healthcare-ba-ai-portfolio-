# Project Charter  
## CityCare General Hospital – HMS Core Modules

**Project Title**: Digital Transformation of Patient Registration, Appointment Scheduling & Billing  
**Project Code**: HMS-CORE-2025  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: August 2025  
**Prepared by**: Business Analyst (Portfolio Simulation)

---

### 1. Project Purpose / Business Need

CityCare General Hospital currently operates with fragmented, largely manual processes for patient registration, appointment management, and billing. This results in:

- Excessive patient wait times
- High rates of duplicate medical records
- Significant appointment no-shows
- Revenue leakage and delayed collections
- Poor real-time visibility for management

The hospital leadership has approved a focused initiative to implement an integrated Hospital Management System covering the three core operational areas.

---

### 2. Project Objectives

| # | Objective | Success Metric | Target |
|---|-----------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | Reduce patient registration time | Average time from arrival to completion | < 3 minutes |
| 2 | Improve data quality | Unique Patient Identification rate | ≥ 95% |
| 3 | Reduce appointment no-shows | No-show percentage | ≤ 15% (from ~23%) |
| 4 | Improve billing accuracy | First-pass clean claim / invoice accuracy | ≥ 90% |
| 5 | Provide operational visibility | Real-time dashboards available | Yes |

---

### 3. High-Level Scope

**In Scope**
- Patient Registration (New & Returning, OPD & IPD)
- Master Patient Index (MPI) with duplicate detection
- Appointment Scheduling, Rescheduling, Cancellation & Reminders
- Billing & Payment Collection (Cash, Card, Basic Insurance)
- Role-based access and audit trails
- Basic operational dashboards & reports

**Out of Scope (Phase 1)**
- Full Electronic Health Record (clinical documentation)
- Pharmacy inventory & dispensing
- Laboratory / Radiology order management
- Advanced claims adjudication & denial management
- Patient portal self-service (Phase 2)
- Telemedicine

### 6. High-Level Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|----------|
| Resistance from front-desk staff | High | Early involvement + training |
| Data migration quality issues | High | Thorough data cleansing rules |
| Integration with legacy systems | Medium | API-first or phased approach |
| Scope creep into clinical modules | Medium | Strict change control |
