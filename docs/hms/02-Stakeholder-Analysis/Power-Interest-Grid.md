# Power-Interest Grid  
## CityCare General Hospital – HMS Project

**Version**: 1.0

---

### Power-Interest Matrix

```
High Power
│
│  Keep Satisfied                  Manage Closely
│  • IT Manager (S04)              • COO / Sponsor (S01)
│  • Consultant Doctors (S09)      • Medical Director (S02)
│                                  • Head of Operations (S03)
│
│──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
│
│  Monitor                         Keep Informed
│  • Nursing Leads (S10)           • Registration Supervisors (S05)
│  • Finance Controller (S11)      • Registration Clerks (S06)
│  • Insurance Coordinators (S13)  • Appointment Coordinators (S07)
│  • Patients (S12)                • Billing Executives (S08)
│
Low Power
         Low Interest ────────────────────────── High Interest
```

---

### Engagement Strategies by Quadrant

| Quadrant | Strategy | Actions |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Manage Closely** | Active management | Weekly steering committee, involve in key decisions, regular one-on-ones |
| **Keep Satisfied** | Maintain satisfaction | Provide progress updates, address concerns quickly, limit unnecessary demands on their time |
| **Keep Informed** | Regular communication | Involve in workshops, demos, UAT; gather feedback; celebrate quick wins |
| **Monitor** | Periodic oversight | Share high-level updates; provide feedback channels; watch for rising interest/power |

---

### Notes

- Registration and Billing staff (Keep Informed) are critical for successful adoption even though their formal power is lower.
- Doctors (Keep Satisfied) can become blockers if the system increases their administrative burden.
- Patients are monitored via feedback surveys and NPS post go-live.
