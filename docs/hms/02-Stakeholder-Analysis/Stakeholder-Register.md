### Stakeholder Categories

**Key Players (High Power + High Interest)**  
S01, S02, S03 → Active management, regular engagement, decision-making involvement.

**Keep Satisfied (High Power + Medium Interest)**  
S04, S09 → Provide updates, address concerns promptly, avoid surprises.

**Keep Informed (Medium Power + High Interest)**  
S05–S08 → Regular communication, involve in requirements validation and UAT.

**Monitor (Low Power + Medium/High Interest)**  
S10–S13 → Periodic updates, feedback channels.

---

### Engagement Notes

- Early involvement of Registration and Billing staff is critical for adoption.
- Doctors need to feel their schedule control is preserved or improved.
- Sponsor requires clear, data-driven progress reporting.
