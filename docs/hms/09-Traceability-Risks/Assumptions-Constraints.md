# Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies  
## CityCare General Hospital – HMS Project

**Version**: 1.0

---

### 1. Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Impact if False |
|----|------------|-----------------|
| A01 | Hospital leadership will actively sponsor and communicate the change | High resistance and low adoption |
| A02 | Subject matter experts (Registration, Billing, Appointment leads) will be available for workshops and UAT | Delayed or incomplete requirements |
| A03 | Existing network and basic hardware at counters are adequate or will be upgraded in time | Performance or usability issues |
| A04 | Price master, doctor schedules, and specialty lists will be provided in clean, agreed format | Delays in configuration |
| A05 | Staff will complete required training before go-live | Poor adoption and higher error rates |
| A06 | SMS/Email gateway can be procured and integrated within project timeline | Reminder functionality delayed |
| A07 | Data protection / privacy requirements are clearly understood and can be met by the selected solution | Compliance risk |

---

### 2. Constraints

| ID | Constraint | Notes |
|----|------------|-------|
| C01 | Project must achieve go-live within approximately 6–7 months | Fixed timeline pressure |
| C02 | Budget is limited to the range approved in the Business Case | Cost control required |
| C03 | Solution must comply with applicable data protection regulations | Non-negotiable |
| C04 | Minimal disruption to daily hospital operations during implementation and training | Phased rollout preferred |
| C05 | System must be usable by non-technical staff with minimal training | Strong usability requirement |
| C06 | Phase 1 scope is strictly limited to Registration, Appointments, and Billing | No clinical modules |

---

### 3. Dependencies

| ID | Dependency | Dependent On | Notes |
|----|------------|--------------|-------|
| D01 | Clean patient data for migration | Hospital data team / current system owners | Critical path |
| D02 | Finalized doctor schedules and price lists | Clinical and Finance teams | Configuration dependency |
| D03 | Notification service availability | IT / Vendor | Required for reminders |
| D04 | User availability for UAT and training | Operations management | Schedule carefully |
| D05 | Infrastructure readiness (if on-premise components) | IT | |
| D06 | Formal sign-off on requirements before build starts | Sponsor and Business Owners | Governance |

---

These assumptions, constraints, and dependencies should be reviewed regularly and updated as the project progresses.
