### Project Summary

**Project Name**: CityCare General Hospital – Core Operational Workflows Digital Transformation  
**Focus Areas**:  
- Patient Registration (OPD & IPD)  
- Appointment Scheduling & Management  
- Billing & Payment Collection  

**Hospital Context**: Mid-sized 250-bed multi-specialty hospital handling 400–500 OPD visits per day.

**Business Problem**:  
Long registration queues, high duplicate patient records, high appointment no-show rates (~22–25%), delayed billing, revenue leakage, and fragmented data across departments.

**Key Outcomes Targeted**:  
- Registration time: 12–18 min → < 3 min  
- Unique patient identification ≥ 95%  
- Appointment no-show reduction by ~40%  
- First-pass billing accuracy ≥ 90%  
- Real-time operational visibility
