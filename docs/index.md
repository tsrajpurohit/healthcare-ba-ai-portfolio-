# Healthcare BA & AI Portfolio

Welcome. This site publishes **Business Analysis and AI Product portfolio case studies** across hospital operations, revenue cycle, clinical research, analytics, and pharmacovigilance.

> All projects are **simulated portfolio case studies** for skill demonstration. They are not based on real commercial client engagements.

## Projects

| Project | Focus |

| **Clinical Trial Management System** | Sites, subjects, visits, compliance – BPMN-style flows, NFR, validation |
| **Healthcare Analytics Dashboard** | KPI dictionary, executive & operational dashboards, Power BI notes |
| **AI-Powered Pharmacovigilance (VigilAI)** | Case processing, literature, signal prioritization – human-in-the-loop AI |
