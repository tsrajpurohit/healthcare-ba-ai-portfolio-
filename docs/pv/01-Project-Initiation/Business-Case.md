# Business Case (Preliminary)  
## VigilAI – AI-Powered Pharmacovigilance Platform

**Version**: 1.0  
**Status**: Early / Day 0–1 view based on sponsor request

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Rising adverse event volumes, global expansion, and increasing regulatory expectations are straining a largely manual pharmacovigilance operating model. The proposed AI-powered platform aims to augment PV professionals by automating repetitive tasks, accelerating case processing, improving signal detection, and providing real-time operational visibility — while preserving human medical judgment and full auditability.

---

### 2. Problem Statement

| Challenge | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| High and growing case volumes | Backlogs, overtime, delayed reporting risk |
| Manual case processing | High cost per case, variability, staff burnout |
| Manual literature screening | Resource intensive; risk of missed relevant safety information |
| Fragmented tracking (Excel + email) | Poor real-time visibility; weak audit trail |
| Traditional signal detection limits | Slower insight; reactive posture |
| Global regulatory complexity | Need for consistency and speed across markets |

---

### 3. Proposed Solution Direction

An AI-enabled pharmacovigilance intelligence and automation layer that works **with** the existing safety database and PV processes to deliver:

1. **Smarter Case Intake & Triage** – Automated capture, duplicate detection, prioritization  
2. **AI-Assisted Case Processing** – Coding suggestions, narrative support, quality checks  
3. **Literature Intelligence** – AI-assisted screening and relevance ranking  
4. **Enhanced Signal Detection** – Faster identification and prioritization of potential signals  
5. **Operational Command Center** – Dashboards for volumes, timelines, quality, signals, workload  
6. **Governance by Design** – Explainability, human-in-the-loop, complete audit trails  

---

### 4. Expected Benefits (Directional)

**Operational**
- Reduction in manual effort per case
- Faster cycle times from receipt to submission-ready
- Better workload distribution via intelligent prioritization

**Safety & Compliance**
- Earlier signal detection
- More consistent processing
- Stronger inspection readiness through transparency and auditability

**Strategic**
- Scalable PV operations supporting global growth
- Higher-value work for skilled PV professionals (more assessment, less administration)
- Foundation for continuous improvement via feedback loops into AI models

---

### 5. Alternatives (High-Level)

| Option | Pros | Cons | View |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Do nothing / hire more FTEs | Familiar | Linear cost growth; does not improve speed/insight fundamentally | Unsustainable |
| Traditional automation (RPA only) | Faster implementation | Limited intelligence; brittle | Partial / complementary |
| Full replacement of safety database | Clean slate | Very high cost, risk, timeline | Not recommended as first step |
| **AI augmentation layer on existing PV ecosystem (Recommended direction)** | Leverages current investment; faster value; lower risk | Requires careful integration & change management | **Preferred** |

---

### 6. Preliminary Recommendation

Authorize discovery and detailed requirements work for an AI-powered pharmacovigilance platform focused on high-ROI, compliance-compatible use cases (case intake/triage, coding assistance, literature screening support, signal prioritization, and operational visibility), with strict human-in-the-loop design.
