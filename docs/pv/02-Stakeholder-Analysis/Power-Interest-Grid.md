### Engagement Notes
- **Manage Closely**: Value owners and AI delivery — regular steering and co-design.
- **Keep Satisfied**: Medical and compliance stakeholders — emphasize control, explainability, and validation.
- **Keep Informed**: Front-line users — heavy involvement in workflow design and UAT so AI feels helpful, not burdensome.
