### Early Insights
- **Sponsor and Operations/Signal leads** are the primary value buyers.
- **Medical Reviewers** must trust AI outputs or adoption will fail — explainability is critical.
- **QA and Compliance** will gate success; involve them from the start.
- **Front-line case processors** need to experience reduced burden, not added complexity.
