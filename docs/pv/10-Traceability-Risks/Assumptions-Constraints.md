# Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies  
## VigilAI

### Assumptions
- Existing safety database remains system of record; VigilAI augments it
- Sufficient historical case data can be made available for model development under appropriate controls
- PV leadership will actively sponsor change management and pilot participation
- Medical reviewers will engage in co-design of AI assistance boundaries
- QA will partner on validation approach rather than only gate at the end
- Priority literature sources can be legally and technically accessed

### Constraints
- Human-in-the-loop required for medical and regulatory-critical decisions
- Explainability and auditability are mandatory
- Phase 1 will not replace the core safety database
- Global regulatory expectations (GVP and local requirements) must be respected
- Data privacy and security controls apply to all PV data

### Dependencies
- Access to safety database interfaces and documentation
- Availability of labeled/historical data for training and testing
- Data Science capacity for model build and MLOps
- IT support for integration and environments
- Subject matter expert time for process workshops and UAT
- Clear decision on pilot products / regions
