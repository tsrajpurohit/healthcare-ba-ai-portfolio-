### Project Summary

**Project Name**: VigilAI – AI-Powered Pharmacovigilance Platform  
**Business Sponsor**: Head of Pharmacovigilance (Global Pharmaceutical Company)  

**Business Request (Day 0)**:  
> "We need an AI-enabled pharmacovigilance platform that can automate safety case processing, improve signal detection, reduce manual workload, and provide better visibility into drug safety operations."

**Current State Challenges**:
- Growing volume of adverse event reports
- Heavy reliance on manual case processing
- Manual literature review
- Excel trackers + email-based workflows
- Increasing global regulatory complexity
- Slow safety signal detection

**Target Outcomes**:
- Faster, more consistent safety case processing
- Earlier and more reliable signal detection
- Significant reduction in manual workload
- Improved operational visibility and compliance posture
- Human-in-the-loop AI design (AI assists, humans decide)
