# Business Case  
## MediClaim AI – Intelligent Revenue Cycle Management Platform

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: August 2025

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Revenue Cycle Management remains one of the highest-cost and highest-leakage areas in healthcare operations. Despite significant investment in traditional RCM systems, many organizations still experience denial rates of 15–25% and Days in AR exceeding 45–55 days.

**MediClaim AI** proposes an AI-augmented RCM platform that uses machine learning to:
- Prevent denials before submission
- Prioritize high-value work
- Assist coding accuracy
- Accelerate cash flow

Conservative estimates show potential annual benefit of **₹4.5 – 8.5 Crore** for a mid-to-large provider system, with payback in 18–24 months.

---

### 2. Problem Statement

| Pain Point | Current Impact |
|------------|----------------|
| High Denial Rate (18–22%) | Significant rework, delayed revenue, staff overtime |
| Low First-Pass Clean Claim Rate (~72%) | High volume of rework and resubmissions |
| Manual Claim Scrubbing | High labor cost, inconsistent quality |
| Reactive Denial Management | Resources spent on preventable issues |
| Unprioritized AR Workqueues | High-value accounts not worked first |
| Coding Variability | Under-coding (revenue loss) and over-coding (compliance risk) |

---

### 3. Proposed Solution

An AI-powered RCM intelligence layer that integrates with existing EHR / Practice Management and Clearinghouse systems to provide:

1. **Pre-Submission Claim Intelligence** – Real-time scrubbing + denial risk score  
2. **Denial Prediction Engine** – Predict likelihood and reason of denial  
3. **Coding Assist** – Suggest ICD-10 / CPT codes with confidence scores  
4. **Smart Workqueues** – AI-ranked AR and denial worklists  
5. **Prior Auth Intelligence** – Status tracking + missing information alerts  
6. **Payment Propensity Insights** – Patient-level payment likelihood scores  

Human-in-the-loop design ensures coders, billers, and denial specialists remain in control.

---

### 4. Expected Benefits (Annualized Estimates)

| Benefit Category | Estimated Annual Value (₹) | Key Driver |
|------------------|----------------------------|----------|
| Reduced Denials & Faster Resubmission | 1.8 – 3.2 Cr | Lower denial rate + higher overturn |
| Improved Clean Claim Rate | 1.2 – 2.0 Cr | Less rework, faster payment |
| Labor Efficiency (Coders / Billers / AR) | 0.9 – 1.6 Cr | 30–40% reduction in manual touches |
| Reduced Revenue Leakage (under-coding) | 0.4 – 0.9 Cr | Better coding suggestions |
| Faster Cash Conversion | Opportunity cost savings | Lower Days in AR |
| **Total Estimated Annual Benefit** | **4.5 – 8.5 Cr** | Conservative range |

**Qualitative Benefits**
- Higher staff satisfaction (less repetitive work)
- Improved compliance posture through consistent rules
- Better payer relationship via cleaner claims
- Data foundation for advanced contract analytics (future)

### 6. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Do Nothing | Zero new cost | Problems persist / worsen | Rejected |
| Traditional RCM Rules Engine only | Lower complexity | Limited predictive power | Insufficient |
| Full RPA Bot Army | Automates repetitive tasks | Brittle, high maintenance | Complementary, not core |
| **AI-Augmented RCM Platform (Selected)** | Predictive + scalable + human-in-loop | Requires data science capability | **Recommended** |

---

### 7. Recommendation

Proceed with development of MediClaim AI focused on high-ROI use cases (Claim Scrubbing, Denial Prediction, Smart Workqueues, Coding Assist). The combination of measurable financial return and strategic positioning in AI-enabled healthcare operations makes this a strong investment.
