### Engagement Notes
- **Manage Closely**: Regular steering, value tracking, decision involvement.
- **Keep Satisfied**: Technical and compliance reviews, avoid surprises.
- **Keep Informed**: Heavy involvement in requirements validation, UAT, and feedback loops — these users determine adoption success.
- **Monitor**: Periodic updates; escalate if interest or influence rises.
