### Stakeholder Insights

- **CFO and RCM Director** are the primary value buyers — focus on measurable financial outcomes.
- **Coders and Denial Specialists** are critical for adoption — AI must feel like a helpful assistant, not a black box.
- **Compliance** must be engaged early on explainability, audit trails, and bias monitoring.
- **IT** cares about clean APIs, security, and maintainability.
