# Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies  
## MediClaim AI

### Assumptions
- Sufficient historical claims and denial data (labeled) are available for model training
- EHR / Practice Management system provides necessary APIs or data extracts
- Clearinghouse integration is feasible within timeline
- RCM leadership will actively sponsor change management
- Coders and billers will participate in pilot and provide feedback
- Organization accepts human-in-the-loop design (not full autonomy in Phase 1)

### Constraints
- Must comply with HIPAA and applicable data protection regulations
- AI cannot have final authority on coding or high-risk claim submission
- Phase 1 timeline and budget boundaries
- Explainability is non-negotiable for user trust and compliance
- Model performance must meet agreed thresholds before broad rollout

### Key Dependencies
- Clean, accessible historical data
- IT resources for integration
- Data Science capacity for model development and monitoring
- End-user availability for co-design and UAT
- Compliance review and sign-off on AI decisioning approach
