# AI Healthcare RCM Platform  
### Flagship case study · AI + Healthcare + BA + Product

!!! note "Portfolio simulation"
    Simulated Business Analysis / AI product case study for learning and demonstration—not a commercial client engagement.

---

## Problem

Revenue cycle teams face rising claim volumes, denials, manual coding effort, and limited prioritization of workqueues. Leadership wants **AI-assisted** capabilities that improve clean-claim rates and staff productivity **without** removing human accountability for final decisions.

---

## My BA / product role

- Frame the business problem and success metrics  
- Map AS-IS RCM workflows and quantify gaps  
- Prioritize AI use cases (impact × feasibility × compliance fit)  
- Define business rules for **human-in-the-loop** AI  
- Produce BRD, AI-oriented FRD, user stories, UAT, and RTM  
- Keep scope realistic for a phased pilot  

---

## Key deliverables

| Area | Artifacts |
|------|-----------|
| Initiation | Charter, business case, scope |
| Stakeholders | Register, RACI, power-interest |
| Process | AS-IS, TO-BE AI-enabled flows, gap analysis |
| AI product | Opportunity map, use-case prioritization, business rules / AI logic |
| Requirements | BRD, functional AI requirements |
| Delivery | User stories, acceptance criteria, sample Jira backlog |
| Quality | UAT scenarios |
| Control | RTM, risk register, assumptions |

---

## Solution focus

AI **augments** intake quality, coding suggestions, denial risk insight, and workqueue prioritization. Deterministic edits and final coding/clinical-financial judgments remain with qualified staff. Explainability and audit logging are first-class requirements.

---

## Intended outcome

A phased pilot definition that reduces manual effort and cycle time, improves prioritization of high-value work, and remains defensible under operational and compliance review.
